## 1. What ASRA Does

Preclinical safety testing relies on animal models, yet high sequence identity between a human target and its animal orthologue does not guarantee equivalent biology across subcellular compartments, cell types, or tissues.

**ASRA (Across-Species Risk Assessment)** evaluates a human target gene against a chosen preclinical comparison species across **four structured biological evidence layers**:

1. **Sequence & Functional Motifs (`sequence`)**
2. **Subcellular Localisation (`localisation`)**
3. **Cell-Type Expression (`cell_expression`)**
4. **Tissue Expression (`tissue_expression`)**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Framing for Judges:** ASRA does **not** claim to retrospectively "predict" historical clinical disasters. In both flagship historical cases (fialuridine in 1993 and TGN1412 in 2006), the definitive mechanistic explanations were published years *after* the clinical trials. ASRA's purpose is to systematically surface cross-species differences that sequence identity alone misses, distinguish **unmeasured** or **unannotated** biology from **biological absence**, and place curated experimental literature alongside live database evidence with traceable citations.

## 2. The Four Biological Evidence Layers

| Layer | Primary Data Sources | What It Measures | Epistemic Safeguards |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. `sequence`** | Ensembl REST (`orthologues`), UniProtKB features, internal motif scanner | 1-to-1 orthology verification, global Needleman–Wunsch protein alignment, residue substitutions overlapping UniProt functional domains/sites, and targeted motif checks (e.g. `SLC29A1` N-terminal `PEXN` motif). | Refuses to guess when orthology is one-to-many or many-to-many (e.g. `CYP3A4` → 9 mouse `Cyp3a` genes); preserves all candidate IDs. |
| **2. `localisation`** | UniProtKB REST (`SUBCELLULAR LOCATION`, ECO evidence codes), Ensembl orthologue xref fallback, `tools/literature.py` | Compares annotated subcellular compartments (`SL-*` IDs) between human and animal proteins, auditing whether each annotation carries experimental (`ECO:0000269` + PubMed), similarity (`ECO:0000250`), or automatic (`ECO:0000256`) support. | Never treats a missing compartment annotation in one species as biological absence; marks `by_similarity` transfers and unreviewed `TrEMBL` entries as `incomplete`. |
| **3. `cell_expression`** | CELLxGENE Discover (WMG v2) for human vs mouse; CELLxGENE Census (`2025-11-08`) recorded audit for macaque; `tools/literature.py` | Compares single-cell RNA detection fractions across ontology-matched (`UBERON` + `CL`) cell populations (`>= 500` cells), or audits dataset-level gene presence (`feature_dataset_presence_matrix`) and donor/assay metadata in Census. | Distinguishes **unmeasured** (gene absent from assay panel or cell population absent from atlas), **undetected** (zero counts), and **biologically absent**. Displays cell-count denominators only when they match the reported percentage. |
| **4. `tissue_expression`** | Human GTEx (`gtex_v8` median TPM) + Animal Bgee (`expressionScore` & call quality) | Matches human and animal tissues by exact or organ-level `UBERON` ontology IDs, pairing human quantitative TPM with animal Bgee expression scores and assay provenance. | Never divides or subtracts GTEx TPM against Bgee scores across incompatible platforms; excludes cultured cell lines (`EFO:*`) from tissue rankings. |

## 3. 60-Second Scientific Explanation: Fialuridine (`SLC29A1`, Human vs Mouse)

- **Clinical Context (McKenzie et al., *NEJM* 1995):** In a 1993 Phase II trial for chronic hepatitis B, 15 patients received fialuridine (FIAU) after preclinical toxicology in mice, rats, dogs, and monkeys showed no hepatotoxicity. At week 13, 7 of 15 patients developed severe hepatotoxicity, lactic acidosis, and pancreatitis; 5 died and 2 survived only after emergency liver transplantation.
- **Why Sequence & Bulk Tissue Expression Alone Are Insufficient:**
  - ASRA's `sequence` layer confirms a 1-to-1 orthologue between human `SLC29A1` (`ENSG00000112759` / `Q99808`, 456 aa) and mouse `Slc29a1` (`ENSMUSG00000023942` / `Q9JIM1`, 460 aa) with **`77.63%` global protein identity**.
  - ASRA's `tissue_expression` layer (`status: ok`) shows both species express `SLC29A1` across **17 matched tissues**, including the liver (human GTEx median `21.48 TPM`; mouse Bgee `expressed`, gold quality, score `85.90` via RNA-Seq, Affymetrix, and in situ hybridisation) and adrenal gland (human `105.77 TPM`; mouse score `93.36`).
- **What ASRA Surfaces in `localisation` and `sequence`:**
  1. **Database Audit (`localisation`, `status: incomplete`, `UniProt 2026_04`):** Canonical UniProtKB annotates human `Q99808` at the **basolateral, apical, and general cell membrane** (`SL-0038`, `SL-0037`, `SL-0039` with experimental PubMed support) and mouse `Q9JIM1` at the **cell membrane** (`SL-0039`, transferred `by_similarity` `ECO:0000250` from `Q99808`). Crucially, **canonical UniProtKB still does not annotate mitochondrial localisation on `Q99808`**.
  2. **Sequence Motif (`sequence`):** ASRA's functional motif check identifies the human N-terminal mitochondrial targeting motif **`PEXN` at residues 71–74 (`PEQN`) in human `Q99808`**, which is disrupted in mouse `Q9JIM1` (**`PAXS` at aligned columns 71–74**).
  3. **Curated Experimental Literature (`tools/literature.py`):** Alongside the UniProt record, ASRA attaches **Lai et al. (*J Biol Chem* 2004)** and **Lee et al. (*J Biol Chem* 2006)**—published 11 and 13 years after the trial—which demonstrated experimentally that human ENT1 localises to the mitochondrial membrane via its N-terminal targeting signal and transports fialuridine into mitochondria, whereas mouse ENT1 lacks this signal and is predominantly plasma-membrane bound. *(Note: Subsequent work by Hendriks et al., *Toxicol Sci* 2019, showed that mitochondrial ENT1 is contributory alongside intramitochondrial phosphorylation by `TK2`.)*

## 4. 30-Second Scientific Explanation: TGN1412 (`CD28`, Human vs Macaque)

- **Clinical Context (Suntharalingam et al., *NEJM* 2006):** Six healthy volunteers received a single `0.1 mg/kg` IV dose of the anti-CD28 superagonist antibody TGN1412 (1/500th of the `50 mg/kg` NOAEL established in cynomolgus macaques). All six developed life-threatening cytokine release syndrome and multiorgan failure within 90 minutes (all six survived after intensive care).
- **Why Sequence & Bulk Tissue Expression Alone Are Insufficient:**
  - ASRA's `sequence` layer shows human `CD28` (`P10747`, 220 aa) and macaque `CD28` (`ENSMMUP00000065245` in rhesus; `ENSMFAP00000034788` in cynomolgus, 220 aa) share **`98.18%` sequence identity** (only 4 substitutions: `F11L, V155A, I175C, V179M`) and **100% conservation of the `MYPPPY` ligand-binding motif**.
  - ASRA's `tissue_expression` layer (`status: ok`) shows conserved bulk expression across lymphoid and mucosal tissues (spleen, lung, colon, liver).
- **What ASRA Surfaces in `cell_expression` (`status: incomplete`):**
  1. **CELLxGENE Census `2025-11-08` Audit (Replayed Recorded Benchmark):** In the human PBMC reference atlas (`ed5d841d-6346-47d4-ab2f-7119ad7e3a35`, Hao et al. 2021, `10x 3' v3`), `CD28` is detected in **`33.37%` (`1,429 / 4,282` primary cells across 8 donors)** of human **`effector memory CD4-positive, alpha-beta T cells (CL:0000905)`**. By contrast, Census `2025-11-08` contains **`0` rhesus macaque `CD4+ effector-memory T cells (CL:0000905, CL:0001087)` and `0` blood/PBMC datasets** (and does not index cynomolgus macaque). ASRA therefore reports the atlas comparison as `inconclusive` rather than inventing animal scRNA-seq numbers.
  2. **Curated Experimental Literature (`Eastwood et al., Br J Pharmacol 2010`):** ASRA attaches the post-trial flow-cytometry finding (published 4 years after the trial) that **human `CD4+ effector-memory T cells (CCR7-)` are `CD28+`**, whereas **both cynomolgus and rhesus macaque `CD4+ effector-memory T cells` are `CD28-`**, explaining why macaques did not mount the `CD4+ TEM`-driven cytokine storm.

## 6. Main Limitations & Defensible Responses to Likely Judges' Questions

### Q1: *"Did ASRA predict the fialuridine or TGN1412 clinical failures?"*
- **Response:** No, and we explicitly avoid claiming retrospective prediction. For fialuridine (1993 failure), human mitochondrial ENT1 localisation and its N-terminal targeting signal were published by Lai et al. (2004) and Lee et al. (2006)—11 to 13 years after the trial. For TGN1412 (2006 failure), the absence of CD28 on macaque CD4+ effector-memory T cells was published by Eastwood et al. (2010)—4 years after the trial. ASRA's value is architectural: it checks the exact biological layers where those interspecies divergences occurred (subcellular localisation, cell-type expression, tissue expression, and sequence motifs), audits database provenance, and surfaces both live database state and curated literature so a translational team is never blinded by a high sequence-identity number (`77.6%` for ENT1, `98.2%` for CD28).

### Q2: *"Why does UniProt not show human ENT1 (`SLC29A1`) in the mitochondrion on its own?"*
- **Response:** Live inspection of UniProt `Q99808` (`UniProt 2026_04`) shows that its canonical `SUBCELLULAR LOCATION` comment only lists `Basolateral cell membrane (SL-0038)`, `Apical cell membrane (SL-0037)`, and `Cell membrane (SL-0039)`. Moreover, mouse `Q9JIM1` is annotated solely `by_similarity` (`ECO:0000250`) transferred from human `Q99808`. That is why ASRA marks the database-only comparison `incomplete`, detects the `PEXN` vs `PAXS` targeting motif difference at residues 71–74 in the sequence layer, and attaches the peer-reviewed experimental literature (`Lai 2004`, `Lee 2006`) as explicitly labelled `LITERATURE` observations with DOIs.

### Q3: *"Why does `cell_expression` return `incomplete` for `CD28` in macaque instead of computing a fold change?"*
- **Response:** In CELLxGENE Census `2025-11-08`, human PBMCs (`Hao et al. 2021`, `ed5d841d-6346-47d4-ab2f-7119ad7e3a35`) have `4,282` primary `CD4+ effector-memory T cells (CL:0000905)` across 8 donors (`33.37%` `CD28+`). However, across all `445,451` rhesus macaque cells in Census `2025-11-08`, there are **zero** blood/PBMC datasets and **zero** cells annotated as `CD4+ effector-memory T cells (CL:0000905 or CL:0001087)`. The only rhesus T cells in Census are generic `CL:0000084` cells in fetal lung (`2e877cd3`, where `CD28` was not even in the measured gene panel: `feature_dataset_presence_matrix = 0`) and adult dorsolateral prefrontal cortex snRNA-seq (`fc6ef080`, `17 / 185` nuclei). Computing a cross-species fold change between adult human blood scRNA-seq and macaque brain nuclear RNA-seq would be scientifically invalid.

### Q4: *"How do you prevent GTEx and Bgee from producing false tissue differences?"*
- **Response:** Human GTEx (`gtex_v8`) reports quantitative bulk RNA-Seq median `TPM`, whereas animal Bgee aggregates multi-experiment RNA-Seq, microarray, EST, and in situ hybridisation into a `0–100` rank `expressionScore` and qualitative presence call. Because `TPM` and `expressionScore` have incompatible units and normalisation, ASRA matches tissues strictly by `UBERON` anatomical ontology IDs and reports both measurements side-by-side without ever computing a ratio or fold change.
